<Callout title="Best Measurement Award">

Measurements are how you show that a system, physical or computational, behaves as
expected, that data are reliable, and that a result actually matters. Strong
contenders pick meaningful targets to measure, capture them precisely through
experimental or computational methods, and report results clearly with appropriate
units or metrics.
