# Solutions Required

# LB
Buffer – stored in the fridge

# 100 mM CaCl2 (Filter sterilized)
**Preparation of 100mM CaCl2 (1000 mL)**
- MW = 110.98 g/mol
- 11.098 g of CaCl2 in 1000 mL H2O mQ

# 100 mM MgCl2 (Filter sterilized)
**Preparation of 100mM MgCl2 (1000 mL)**
- MW = 203.30 g/mol
- 20.33 g of MgCl2 in 1000 mL H2O mQ

# 85 mM CaCl2, 15% Glycerol
**Preparation (20 mL)**
- 17 mL 100mM CaCl2
- 3 mL 100% glycerol
- Filter sterilize

> Everything should be kept on ice. Open the tube and discard supernatant inside the hood.

---

# Protocol

1. Grow 5 mL overnight cultures of required cell (e.g. DH5α, BL21(DE3), etc.)

2. Add 1/100 volume of your overnight culture to fresh growth media: 2 mL overnight into 200 mL fresh media

3. Grow culture to an OD600 = 0.4

4. Split culture into 4 falcon tubes (50 mL each) and pellet at 4000 g for 15 mins at 4°C (discard supernatant)

5. Resuspend pellets in a (total) 50 mL cold MgCl2 (12.5 mL into each of the 4 falcons) by gently tapping the falcons on the palm (avoid pipetting up and down)

6. Centrifuge again at 4000 g for 15 mins at 4°C (discard supernatant)

7. Resuspend pellets in (total) 50 mL cold CaCl2 (12.5 mL into each of the 4 falcons) by gently tapping the falcons on the palm (avoid pipetting up and down)

8. Place tubes on ice 30 min.

9. Centrifuge again at 4000 g for 15 mins at 4°C (discard supernatant)

10. Combine and resuspend pellets in 10 mL 85 mM CaCl2, 15% glycerol (filter sterilized) (2.5 mL into each of the 4 falcons & then combine) by pipetting gently up and down

11. Centrifuge again at 4000 g for 15 mins at 4°C

12. Resuspend pellets again in 2 mL 85 mM CaCl2, 15% glycerol (filter sterilized) by pipetting gently up and down

13. Aliquot 50 μL each, into sterile 1.5 mL eppendorfs (keep on dry ice)

14. Freeze and store at -80°C
