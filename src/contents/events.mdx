Our first official event as the iGEM Team 2026 was the Group T Jobfair on February 17th, 2026. Still in the recruitment phase we knew that Group T, which is the engineering and entrepreneurship campus of KU Leuven, could bring us the ideal computer science addition to our team. <EventEntry title="Humasol Fair" date="February 2026" location="KU Leuven" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

On 23 February 2026, our team members Martha, Azra, Sarah, and Alec attended the Impact Jobfair organised by Humasol and i2im at University Hall in Leuven. This event allowed us to meet and communicate with newly graduated students and professionals. It was a great opportunity to expand our network and additionally support our fellow Technovation Hub Team, Humasol, who are working on renewable energy solutions in Asia, Africa and South America. Reaching around 40 people, consisting of professionals and new graduates, Marie, Elizabeth and Sarah gave brief presentations about “EMPOWER” while also attending workshops all around pitching and entrepreneurship.



</EventEntry>

<EventEntry title="STEM University Workshop" date="March 2026" location="KU Leuven" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

The STEM University Workshop was our first touchpoint with science communication to a younger audience. We worked with two groups of approximately 20–30 students throughout the day, where we gave short introduction to biotechnology in Dutch, followed by an interactive kiwi DNA extraction experiment in which the students could participate. Each session was concluded with a quiz based on the presentation, allowing us to reinforce the concepts in an engaging way. Milica, Esma, Azra, and Tim safely guided students in their experiments and communicated with 40 students in total.




</EventEntry>

<EventEntry title="VTK Jobfair" date="March 2026" location="KU Leuven" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

On 12 March 2026, we attended the VTK Job Fair, where we explored companies active in engineering and technology while also representing our own team and project. Team members Esma and Azra reached 60 students in total and promoted recruitment to the team.

<EventEntry title="LBK Jobfair" date="March 2026" location="KU Leuven" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

LBK, short for Leuvense Bio-ingenieurskring and founded in 1877, is a student society for bioengineering at KU Leuven. The LBK Jobfair connected Elizabeth and Robin from our EMPOWER team with representatives from different biotech companies and like-minded science students. Similar to the Group T Jobfair, we used the opportunity to recruit members for our team that could help in the WIKI design and development, reaching about 100 people.


</EventEntry>

<EventEntry title="iGEM workshop under Technovaction Hub" date="March 2026" location="KU Leuven Campus Geel" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

On 26 March 2026, Brandon, Martha and Sarah had the pleasure of organising a workshop for Technovation Hub at the KU Leuven Campus Geel. In an interactive setting we let 10 third-year undergraduate students of biotechnology brainstorm iGEM-like projects, which they had to defend in a mock judging session. The event helped us improve our science communication skills for higher technical levels, critically evaluate other projects, and encouraged valuable exchange with other students. We are hoping we could inspire some young students to take on big challenges with synthetic biology and even join an iGEM team.



</EventEntry>

For the third consecutive year, our team attended the BFH iGEM meetup in Hamburg, held from 15–17 May and bringing together 25 teams from across Europe. 
Alongside practice presentations in preparation for the Grand Jamboree in Paris, the weekend included project presentations from participating teams and insights shared by previous Grand Prize winners, including iGEM Brno and iGEM Munich. 
The team's poster was recognised with the Best Poster Prize, and the event offered the opportunity to connect with the wider European iGEM community ahead of the Grand Jamboree.

<EventEntry title="Munster Mini Jamboree" date="July 2026" location="Münster, GER" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

<EventEntry title="Malaga iGEM Meetup" date="July 2026" location="Malaga, ESP" summary="Replace with a one-sentence teaser for this event.">

Our team attended the University of Málaga's European Meet Up from 14–16 July, an event bringing together iGEM teams from across Europe and beyond. The programme included talks on AI and innovation, along with networking and team-building activities involving iGEM students from multiple countries.
Beyond the formal programme, the team explored Málaga alongside local iGEM students from iGEM UMA, including local landmarks, and took part in city-wide activities with other participating teams and faculty. Team members Azra, Sarah, Elizabeth and Marie have reached 7 other iGEM teams. The team's engagement was recognised with two awards: Elizabeth received the Best Speaker prize, and the team won the Bingo prize for completing the most activities with other students and faculty across the city.
The event offered the opportunity to connect with the broader European iGEM community, exchange perspectives on synthetic biology and innovation, and build relationships with fellow teams outside a competition context.
