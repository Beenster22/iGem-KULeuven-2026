Throughout the season, our team took part in talks, workshops, meetups, and outreach
activities that shaped our project and connected us with the wider community. This page
is a running timeline of those events — click any entry to read more about it.

<Callout title="Adding an event">

Duplicate an `<EventEntry>` below for each new event. `images` takes an array of photo
URLs uploaded via [the uploads tool](https://teams.igem.org/go/deliverables/wiki/uploads)
(e.g. `images={["https://static.igem.wiki/teams/2026/kuleuven/events/example-1.jpg"]}`) —
the collapsed card cycles through them, and the expanded view shows the full set.
Leave `images` off entirely if you don't have photos yet.
