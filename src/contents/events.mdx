<EventEntry title="BFH iGEM Meetup" date="May 2026" location="Hamburg, GER">

**What happened** — For the third consecutive year, our team attended the BFH iGEM meetup in Hamburg, held from 15–17 May and bringing together 25 teams from across Europe. 
Alongside practice presentations in preparation for the Grand Jamboree in Paris, the weekend included project presentations from participating teams and insights shared by previous Grand Prize winners, including iGEM Brno and iGEM Munich. 
The team's poster was recognised with the Best Poster Prize, and the event offered the opportunity to connect with the wider European iGEM community ahead of the Grand Jamboree.
