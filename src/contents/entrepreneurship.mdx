## Development

### Marketing and brand identity

#### Why brand identity matters for EMPOWER

Our research revealed a clear gap in current PMOS communication: existing resources tend to fall
into one of two categories — either highly scientific and difficult to access, or accessible but
lacking scientific depth — rarely both. Verity, a UK-based PMOS charity, offers strong medical
credibility, but its visual identity feels distant and less
approachable[ref: Verity. (n.d.). Verity: Sharing the truth about PMOS. Retrieved September 7, 2026, from https://www.verity-pcos.org.uk/].
PCOS Challenge, the largest PMOS patient advocacy and awareness organization globally, builds a
strong sense of community and warmth, but with less scientific
grounding[ref: PCOS Challenge: The National Polycystic Ovary Syndrome Association. (n.d.). Retrieved September 7, 2026, from https://pcoschallenge.org/].
Other iGEM teams, meanwhile, produce high-quality research that rarely gets translated for a
broader, non-scientific audience.

No existing organization combines scientific credibility, accessibility, and a strong visual
identity all at once. This is the space EMPOWER's brand is designed to fill: not simply to look
professional, but to be credible, approachable, and trusted.

#### Research foundation

Before beginning the design process, we conducted desk research and a competitor analysis to
understand how people currently experience PMOS-related health information. A key finding was
that reliable information often exists but remains difficult to understand or trust for those
seeking it.

This insight guided the development of EMPOWER's brand identity through two classic brand
archetypes: the **Sage**, who builds trust through knowledge, expertise, and clear explanation,
and the **Caregiver**, who builds trust through empathy, reassurance, and genuine support.
Combining both means creating a brand that is credible enough to be trusted scientifically, yet
warm enough to feel approachable. In this way, EMPOWER bridges scientific research and health
communication without compromising either scientific depth or human connection.

#### Client validation & sign-off process

A strong brand isn't built in one go. It's built step by step, with regular checkpoints where the
people commissioning the design review the work and give their approval before moving on to the
next stage. This is standard practice in professional branding and design, and it's exactly how
EMPOWER's identity was developed. The iGEM KU Leuven team acted as the client throughout the
process, reviewing and approving the brand at seven key moments. This ensured that important
decisions — from the overall direction to the logo concept and final colors — were never made in
isolation. Instead, each decision was discussed and confirmed together before moving on to the
next stage.

#### The brand in action

A brand only really proves itself once people start using it. EMPOWER's identity is already out
in the world in several ways: a two-minute video that brings the brand to life through motion and
sound; a poster using this identity that won Best Poster at the BFH international meet-up in
Germany — real external proof that the design choices work, not just that they look nice on
screen; and the identity applied across our team hoodies, t-shirts, and stickers, giving EMPOWER a
consistent, recognizable look wherever people encounter it.
