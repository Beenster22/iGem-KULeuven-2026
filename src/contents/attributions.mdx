## Attributions

Besides crediting the work of others, we believe that addressing our use of AI is just as important. In our AI Tool Use section of the attribution form, we have listed every AI tool we used, whether to take tasks off our plate or to speed up our work. AI served as a support tool throughout the project: all AI-generated content was reviewed by us, all writing on this wiki is our own with AI used mainly to correct grammar, and every conclusion, especially when analyzing results, was drawn by us. AI was also used to help us code this wiki, assisting with implementation while every design and content decision remained ours.
