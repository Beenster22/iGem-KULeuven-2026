## Antibiotic susceptibility (MIC)

Minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) of _P. vulgatus_ DSM1447 (wild-type) and RC1806 against a panel of
antibiotics. Bars show the determined MIC, individual points are the raw replicate measurements, and hatched
bars mark antibiotics for which no MIC was found up to the highest concentration tested. Use the toggle to
switch between the two cutoffs (90% or 85% growth reduction relative to the untreated control) used to call
an MIC value.
