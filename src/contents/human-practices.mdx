## Interviews

<Callout title="Adding an interview">

Group interviews into an `<HPSection title="...">` per stakeholder category (e.g. patients,
clinicians, researchers) — each one gets its own heading, a scrollable carousel of the people
interviewed, and a context blurb underneath. Duplicate an `<Interviewee>` inside a section for
each person; `image` takes a photo URL uploaded via
[the uploads tool](https://teams.igem.org/go/deliverables/wiki/uploads) (leave it off to show
initials instead) and the interviewee's `children` is the full write-up shown in their popup
under "Interview Summary". Any plain paragraph text written inside `<HPSection>` alongside the
`<Interviewee>` entries — like this callout's neighbor below — becomes that section's context
blurb, shown under the carousel.
